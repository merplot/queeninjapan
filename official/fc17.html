Vol. 17 — Autumn 1980

A relatively rare letter from Freddie is translated in the front. There are several news items of interest, including the huge success of Another One Bites the Dust on the US charts, and an announcement of the upcoming US tour, info on Flash Gordon, and a cafe in Shibuya called "Idol House" that was holding a Queen film exhibition and offered 10% off to fanclub members. There is also an interesting blurb regarding Chris Rea's new album Tennis, claiming that Roger played a Stratocaster on the record, although there are no specific tracks mentioned. While it's known that Roger and Chris Rea were friends, he doesn't seem to be listed in the credits on the album, so I'm unsure if this is fact or fiction.
