Vol. 22 — July 1982

This issue contains lots of info for Japanese fans regarding the Jun 5th, 1982 Milton Keynes show. Planned dates for the upcoming North American tour are mentioned as well, including mention of a planned show in Hawaii on September 17th, 1982. Hawaii is a popular vacation destination for Japanese tourists, given its proximity to Japan and the amount of Americans of Japanese descent living there. Because of this, the fanclub was planning a group trip to see the Hawaii show for a cost of ¥185,000 per person. Ultimately it seems this Hawaii show never came to be—I have not seen it mentioned on any gigographies nor concert itineraries, and furthermore I don't see any mention of this proposed fanclub trip in any future fanclub magazine issues.

Another point of interest is a 1982 European tour itinerary in the back, which lists two dates for Frankfurt: April 28th & 29th. According to queenlive.ca, the April 29th date never ended up happening either, but this itinerary may have been what started the rumor. There is also a list of dates and attendance numbers for the 1981 South American tour, but for some reason the first date in Buenos Aires is omitted.

It's noted that there's a Queen tenth anniversary exhibit being held at the Parco Shibuya department store. Parco would also sponsor Queen's 1982 Japanese tour later in the year.
