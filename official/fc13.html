Vol. 13 — Autumn 1978

Vol. 13 is relatively light in content—its main feature is a first listen of Jazz, which was newly released in Japan, with some choice fan club members and staff. There's also a message from Roger as well as a color photospread of the band at the release press conference overseas. Our good nakayoshi Yuri Kitagawa provides a dynamic illustration in the vein of Manken Queen (which she also contributed to), a visual representation of Fight From the Inside. Kitagawa was drawing professionally by this point, having works serialized in Hana to Yume and more.
