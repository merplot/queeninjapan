Shadow Queen was begun by a high school student in 1992 and ran until March 1999, when the group was temporarily disbanded before being resurrected by new leadership under the name "Partners & Crime", named after their publication (which was released three times a year). This iteration seemed to have run through the 2000s, possibly into the early 2010s. This group also ran a website, and even hosted an English-language version at one point. Fan events and meetups were held roughly once a year in Tokyo, Kyoto, and Nagoya. As the official Japanese fanclub was disbanded in the early 90s, this group seems to have been a way for Japanese fans to keep in touch with each other and follow the careers of the remaining bandmembers throughout the 90s and 2000s. Every November, the club held a drive to collect funds for the Mercury Phoenix Trust, the charity begun by Brian, Roger, and Queen manager Jim Beach to combat AIDS.

The Shadow Queen/Partners & Crime fanclub is unique in that it was put together at a time when the band was winding down activities due to Freddie's death in 1991. That there were active fanclubs and meetups throughout the 90s is a testament to the passion of the Japanese fanbase.
